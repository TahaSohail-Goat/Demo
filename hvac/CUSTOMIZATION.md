## High-risk claims to verify

Do not publish licenses, insurance, review counts, star ratings, years in business, awards, guaranteed response times, same-day promises, 24/7 availability, financing rates, discounts, warranties, manufacturer-authorized status, or BBB status without current evidence and client approval.

## Form and booking setup

`contact.html` contains a static demo form. JavaScript deliberately prevents network submission. Replace the demo handler in `js/main.js` with the selected CRM, form endpoint, or booking workflow, then add spam protection, server-side validation, error states, success handling, consent storage, notifications, and conversion tracking.

Potential integrations include ServiceTitan, Housecall Pro, Jobber, an email form service, or a custom CRM. Use provider documentation and never commit credentials.

## Analytics and call tracking

Important links contain `data-cta` attributes such as `header-call`, `hero-request-service`, `mobile-call`, `maintenance-plan`, `replacement-estimate`, and `contact-form-submit`. Attach approved analytics events later. Replace every displayed and linked phone number consistently if a tracking provider is used.

## Photography

Real business photography will make the strongest prospect version. Prefer the actual owner, technicians, vehicles, equipment, shop, and recognizable service-area settings. Optimize below-the-fold images, keep width/height attributes, write useful alt text, and avoid unlicensed stock assets.

## Before sending a personalized link

Preview the homepage, all navigation links, at least three direct service URLs, the form, mobile menu, mobile bottom bar, phone links, FAQ, footer, and 320–1440 px layouts. Confirm that the URL opens the HVAC site directly rather than the collection directory.
