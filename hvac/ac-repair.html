What Is Your AC Doing?

Cooling problems can have many causes. A focused system evaluation helps identify what is happening before repair options are discussed.

  • Blowing warm air
  • Weak airflow
  • System will not turn on
  • Frequent cycling
  • Unusual noises
  • Thermostat problems
  • Water around equipment
  • Higher energy usage

Common Cooling Problems

Airflow restrictions, controls, electrical components, refrigerant circuits, drainage, and equipment condition can all affect cooling. A technician should evaluate the system rather than relying on one symptom alone.

Repair or Replacement?

A major repair is a good moment to compare cost, repair history, comfort, efficiency, and the condition of the overall system. Summit Comfort can organize those considerations without presenting one universal answer.
