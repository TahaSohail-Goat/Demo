When to visit

Blossom in April. Gold in October.

Mar–Apr

Apricot and cherry blossom across the terraces. Cool nights; bring layers.

May–Sep

Warm days, open passes and the longest light for glacier trips.

Oct–Nov

Poplars turn gold and the air is clearest for views of the peaks.
