Questions

Things people ask before their first visit.

Does getting an implant hurt?
The area is fully numbed, so you'll feel pressure rather than pain. Most patients take over-the-counter pain relief for a day or two afterwards. Sedation is available if you're anxious.
How long does the whole implant process take?
Placement takes about an hour. The implant then bonds with the bone for three to six months, and the final crown is fitted in a short visit. You'll wear a temporary tooth in between.
Can I pay in instalments?
Yes. Any treatment over $500 can be spread over 12 months at 0% APR, subject to a quick eligibility check at reception.
Do you see nervous patients?
Every day. Tell us when you book and we'll allow extra time, explain each step before it happens and agree a stop signal with you.
What if I have a dental emergency?
We hold emergency slots every day. Call before 11 am and we'll aim to see you the same day.
