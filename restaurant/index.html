Verdell · Seasonal tasting kitchen

Seven courses, plated in front of you.

A 42-seat dining room built around an open kitchen. The menu follows eleven farms and one coastline, and changes every month.

Reserve a table See this month's menu
I — The plate

Thrown by hand, two streets away.

Every plate comes from a potter on Kiln Lane. No two are the same shape, so no two courses look quite alike.

II — The sauce

Saffron beurre blanc, whisked to order.

Shallot, white wine and cold butter, mounted at the pass the moment your table is called. It never sits in a pan.

III — The scallop

Ninety seconds a side.

Hand-dived that morning and seared in brown butter until the crust is three millimetres deep and the centre is barely set.

IV — The garden

Pea shoots, viola, sea salt.

Picked at 4 pm by the grower, on the pass by 6. The last thing on the plate, and the first thing you taste.

Visiting

Good to know before you come.

Can you cater for dietary requirements?+
Yes. Every course has a vegetarian version, and we can work around most allergies if you tell us when you book. We can't guarantee a nut-free kitchen.
Is there a dress code?+
No. Come as you are. Most guests dress smart-casual.
Can we bring children?+
Children over 10 are welcome for the tasting menu. Younger guests are very welcome at the bar before 19:00.
What if I need to cancel?+
Cancel or change up to 48 hours before at no charge. Inside 48 hours we charge $50 per guest, as we buy produce for every booked seat.
Do you sell gift vouchers?+
Yes, for any amount or for a full tasting menu with pairing. Ask at the bar or call us.
