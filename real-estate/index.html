Selling with Calder Hale

A sale, choreographed.

Four deliberate stages, one senior advisor and a weekly report you can actually read. Most of our homes go to contract within six weeks of launch.

  1. 01 · Week one

    Private valuation

    A walkthrough, a comparative market analysis and a pricing strategy grounded in closed sales, not hope.

  2. 02 · Weeks two to three

    Preparation & staging

    Our designers edit, repair and stage, so every room photographs, films and tours at its best.

  3. 03 · Launch

    Film, photography & debut

    A cinematic listing, private previews for qualified buyers, then a coordinated public launch.

  4. 04 · Offer to keys

    Negotiation & close

    Clear advice on every offer, and a calm, managed escrow through to the final walkthrough.
