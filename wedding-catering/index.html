FAQ

Questions families ask first.

How much advance do you take?
30% to lock the date, 50% a week before the function and the rest on the day. Everything is on a written quote.
Do you follow Punjab's one-dish rule?
Yes. For functions in Punjab we plan the menu within the provincial rules for the venue and city, and we'll tell you exactly what's allowed before you confirm.
Can overseas families plan with you remotely?
Most of our Baraat bookings come from families abroad. We hold video calls in your time zone, send decor previews and tasting videos, and a coordinator shares photos from every setup.
Is the food halal and hygienic?
All meat is halal and sourced from the same suppliers every week. Our kitchen is inspected by the food authority and you're welcome to visit it.
What if guest numbers change?
Confirm final numbers 72 hours before. We cook 5% extra so nobody goes without.
