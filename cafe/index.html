Morrow Coffee · Roasters & café

Good mornings, roasted on Tuesdays.

Specialty coffee we roast ourselves, fresh bakes from 7 am and a table you can stay at. Scroll to follow one bean all the way to your cup.

See the menuFind a café
01 · Origin

Every bean starts green.

This one grew at 2,100 m in Guji, Ethiopia. Picked ripe, dried on raised beds, shipped to us raw.

02 · Roast

Eleven minutes in the drum.

We roast in small batches every Tuesday. Around 196 °C the beans crack like popcorn, and that's where the flavour is decided.

03 · Extract

18 grams in. 36 out.

Ground fresh for every shot and pulled in about 28 seconds. Your barista weighs every one.

04 · Pour

Steamed to 60 °C, poured by hand.

Oat, almond or whole milk, all the same price. The rosetta is free.
