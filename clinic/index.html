FAQ

Before you come in.

Do I need a referral?+
Not for most departments. You can book a GP, pediatrician, dermatologist or women's health appointment directly. Some insurers ask for a GP referral for cardiology and orthopedics, and our reception team can check this for you.
Which insurance do you accept?+
We work with most major insurers and corporate health plans, and offer fixed self-pay prices for everyone else. Bring your insurance card and we'll confirm your cover at check-in.
Can I walk in without an appointment?+
Yes. Urgent care is open 8 am to 10 pm every day, and the live wait time is shown at the top of this page. For a life-threatening emergency, call your local emergency number.
How do I get my test results?+
Results appear in the patient app as soon as your doctor has reviewed them, with a short note explaining what they mean. You can also request a printed copy at reception.
Is there parking?+
Free underground parking for patients, with step-free lift access to every floor.
